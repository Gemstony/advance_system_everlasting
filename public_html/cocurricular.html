Co-Curricular & Talent Development

Leadership, Creativity & Physical Excellence

A rich programme of sports, arts, debate and innovation clubs to cultivate leadership, resilience and practical skills beyond the classroom.

Sports & Athletics Programs

Structured training across team and individual sports with professional coaching, fitness conditioning and competitive fixtures.

Builds discipline, teamwork and physical excellence while creating talent pathways for regional and national competitions.

Music, Drama & Fine Arts Clubs

Comprehensive arts provision including ensemble music, theatre productions and studio-based visual arts instruction.

Encourages creative expression, cultural literacy and performance skills through showcases and external examinations.

Debate & Public Speaking Clubs

Regular coaching in argumentation, rhetoric and persuasive presentation with inter-school competitions and mentorship.

Strengthens critical thinking, confidence and leadership presence essential for future civic and professional roles.

Science & Innovation Clubs

Project-based STEM clubs focusing on experimentation, robotics and applied problem-solving with competition opportunities.

Develops inquiry, prototyping skills and an innovation mindset through mentor-led projects and science fairs.

Community Outreach & Leadership Training

Service-learning initiatives and leadership workshops that connect students to community projects and civic responsibility.

Produces ethical leaders with practical project management and community engagement experience.

Enrichment Programs (Culinary, Coding, Tailoring, Languages)

Hands-on vocational and creative workshops designed to teach marketable practical skills alongside academic study.

Prepares students for entrepreneurship and applied careers while fostering creativity and technical competence.

Why Co-Curricular Development Matters

Everlasting Academy believes that excellence extends beyond examinations. Our co-curricular programme intentionally develops leadership, resilience and creative intelligence through sustained practice, expert coaching and public performance. These experiences translate into stronger academic focus, higher self-efficacy and employable skills.
