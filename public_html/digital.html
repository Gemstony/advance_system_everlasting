Digital & Learning Technology

21st-Century Learning Infrastructure & Digital Services

A strategic digital ecosystem combining secure platforms, modern labs and teacher development to elevate learning outcomes and institutional transparency.

Computer Labs & ICT Training

Multiple purpose-built computer labs with modern workstations and scheduled ICT curricula for students and staff.

Regular competency assessments ensure graduates leave with practical digital skills for study and employment.

E-Learning Platform & Digital Content

A secure learning management system providing curriculum-aligned digital resources, recorded lessons and assessment tools.

Centralised student assessment platform delivering timely reports, performance dashboards and analytics for stakeholders.

Supports independent learning, citation practice and access to global reference materials.
