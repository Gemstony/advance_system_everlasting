About Everlasting Academy

Everlasting Academy is a private boarding secondary school based in Misugusugu, Kibaha — committed to academic excellence, character formation, and practical skills for the 21st century.

  1. Home
  2. About
School Motto
"Where every student thrives"

Everlasting Academy places every learner at the center of development — academically, socially and morally.

Core identity
Private boarding school — Misugusugu, Kibaha
Mission Statement

"To provide quality education that offers a balanced physical, mental, moral, social and vocational education in order to produce dependable and responsible citizens."

School Vision

To be a leading educational institution in East Africa nurturing the mental, physical and social faculties for self-reliance.

Our Commitment

We commit to personalised mentorship, rigorous assessments and practical enrichment that prepare students for tertiary study and responsible citizenship.

Our Approach

A holistic learning model combining strong academic foundations with vocational skills and character formation, delivered in a secure boarding environment.

At Everlasting Academy we integrate evidence-based pedagogy, targeted exam preparation and hands-on enrichment — ensuring students graduate resilient, capable and ready for modern challenges.
