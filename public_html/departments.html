ORDINARY LEVEL PROGRAMME (Form I – IV)

Mastery Under the Revised Curriculum Framework

The Ordinary Level programme provides a comprehensive academic foundation structured according to the national curriculum requirements. Students undertake compulsory core subjects alongside selected electives that develop analytical, scientific, linguistic, and commercial competence..

1. Physics Education

Master fundamental mechanics, energy and electricity through theory and guided laboratory work. Builds quantitative reasoning and problem-solving for science and engineering pathways.

2. Chemistry Education

Learn atomic structure, reactions and safe laboratory practice with clear experimental reporting. Prepares students for analytical thinking required in health, environment and chemical fields.

3. Biology Education

Study cell biology, physiology and ecology with practical lab and field observation. Develops scientific method skills relevant to health and life-science careers.

4. Integrated Science Practicals

Hands-on experiments emphasise measurement, error analysis and scientific reporting. Strengthens practical competence for national practical assessments.

Mathematics & Analytical Studies

This area of study is designed to strengthen students’ logical reasoning, quantitative skills, and problem-solving abilities. It forms the backbone of the Ordinary Level programme by ensuring learners can approach challenges with precision and analytical depth.

1. Mathematics Test Preparation

Designed to sharpen logical precision and fluency for success in school tests, inter-regional competitions, and national examinations.

2. Problem-Solving & Logical Reasoning

Structured exercises and drills develop adaptable reasoning strategies. Enhances exam resilience and cognitive flexibility.

3. Applied Mathematics Skills

Applies mathematics to real contexts — finance, measurements and simple models. Supports entrepreneurship and commerce subjects with practical numeracy.

4. Classroom Study & Hands-On Practices

Emphasizes active learning through guided exercises, collaborative problem-solving, and real-world applications.

Language & Communication Studies

Comprehensive development of linguistic precision, articulation, and intercultural fluency across academic and professional contexts. Cultivates confident expression, persuasive dialogue, and refined communication for leadership and global engagement.

1. Mandarin (Chinese) Language Studies

Structured learning of spoken Mandarin, character writing and cultural foundations. Builds global communication skills for international trade, diplomacy and academic exchange.

2. English Language Mastery

Focused training in grammar, comprehension and academic composition. Strengthens clarity of expression for school and higher study.

3. Public Speaking & Debate Skills

Regular practice in speechcraft and structured debates builds persuasive confidence. Develops leadership presence and civic engagement.

4. Academic Writing Skills

Practical lessons in essay structure, referencing and report-writing. Prepares students for rigorous Advanced Level coursework.

Social & Civic Studies

Explores history, geography, governance, and ethical responsibility to build informed, responsible citizens. Develops analytical thinking, civic awareness, and leadership skills for community and national impact.

1. History Studies (historiya ya Tanzania na Maadili)

SCritical study of national and global history with source evaluation. Promotes analytical thinking about cause, consequence and context.

2. Geography Studies

Physical and human geography plus map-work and spatial awareness. Prepares students for environmental and planning-related fields.

3. Civics & Citizenship Education

Teaches governance, rights and civic responsibility through project work. Cultivates ethical, community-minded leadership.

4. Environmental Studies

Sustainability themes and practical local conservation projects. Encourages stewardship and applied environmental thinking.

Commercial & Technology Studies

Covers trade, entrepreneurship, financial literacy, and ICT skills to equip students for modern commerce. Builds practical business acumen, digital competence, and innovative problem-solving for real-world applications.

1. Commerce Education

Fundamentals of trade, markets and small business operations. Introduces entrepreneurial thinking and commercial awareness.

2. Bookkeeping & Financial Literacy

Practical double-entry basics, budgeting and personal finance skills. Equips students for responsible fiscal decision-making.

3. Computer Studies

Core ICT skills: word processing, spreadsheets and basic programming logic. Builds digital fluency for academics and enterprise.

4. Entrepreneurship Foundations

Idea development, simple business plans and pitch practice. Encourages feasible start-up thinking and initiative.

Physical Sciences (PCM)

In-depth study of physics, chemistry and higher mathematics, emphasising laboratory work and analytical modelling. Prepares students for engineering, physics and applied-mathematics pathways.

Biological Sciences (PCB / CBG)

Advanced biology with applied chemistry, fieldwork and rigorous laboratory research. Prepares students for medicine, biotechnology and environmental science careers.

Social Sciences (HGL / EGM)

Study of history, geography and economics with emphasis on policy analysis and research methods. Equips students for law, governance, development and social-research roles.

Business & Economics Track

Advanced economics, business strategy, accounting and entrepreneurship modules with case-based learning. Trains students for finance, management and enterprise leadership.

STEM Innovation Track

Applied computing, statistics and technology-driven projects that foster design thinking and prototype development. Ideal for data science, software engineering and tech entrepreneurship.

A-Level Combinations

PCM: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics — Engineering & Physical Sciences.
PCB: Physics, Chemistry, Biology — Medicine & Life Sciences.
CBG: Chemistry, Biology, Geography — Environmental & Health Sciences.
HGL: History, Geography, Language — Law, Education & Humanities.
EGM: Economics, Geography, Mathematics — Economics, Planning & Finance.
ECA: Economics, Commerce, Accounting — Business & Finance pathways.
