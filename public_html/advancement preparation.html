Academic Advancement & Preparatory Programs

Preparatory Pathways — Pre-Form 1 & Pre-Form 5

Structured foundation and bridging modules that strengthen readiness, boost confidence and improve placement outcomes for secondary and advanced-level entry.

Pre-Form 1 Foundation Program

Targeted courses in English, Mathematics, study skills and discipline orientation to secure a confident and successful start to secondary school.

Pre-Form 5 Bridging Program

Subject-combination alignment, advanced concept revision and analytical practice that ensure students choose the right A-Level pathway and enter prepared.

Remedial & Support Classes

Small-group clinics and one-to-one tutoring targeting identified gaps; personalised learning plans accelerate mastery of essential topics.

Holiday Academic Camps & Revision Retreats

Intensive, curriculum-aligned sessions with examiner-style practice and feedback to build exam readiness and confidence.

Examination Preparation Clinics

Regular mock examinations, timed past-paper practice and analytics-driven review to sharpen exam technique and time management.

Study Skills & Time Management Workshops

Practical training in effective study routines, note-taking, revision planning and stress-management for sustained academic growth.
