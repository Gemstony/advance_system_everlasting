Core Enrichment Tracks

Each track blends practical workshops, theory sessions and real-world projects so students graduate with marketable skills and professional confidence.

Culinary Arts & Professional Cooking

Intensive, hands-on culinary training covering kitchen safety, knife skills and menu execution.

Students practice international and local cuisines, plate presentation and small-scale food enterprise management.

Tailoring & Fashion Design

Practical garment construction, pattern drafting and sewing techniques taught in modern studios.

Coursework includes fashion illustration, small-batch production and market-ready collection development.

Bakery & Pastry Arts

Professional training in breads, pastries, cake finishing and hygiene standards for commercial baking.

Students learn recipe scaling, cost control and product display for retail and catering services.

Mandarin (Chinese) Language Program

Structured language pathway from fundamentals to conversational competence and basic literacy in Mandarin.

Includes cultural modules, practical communication exercises and pathways to international exchange opportunities.

Coding & Software Development

Project-based programming courses in web and app development, version control and algorithmic thinking.

Students build portfolios through team projects, hackathons and industry mentorship placements.

Business Education & Entrepreneurship

Applied business modules covering financial literacy, marketing, business planning and micro-enterprise operations.

Students develop viable business proposals, pitch to panels and access seed-linkages for student ventures.

Program Structure & Industry Linkages

Each enrichment track combines studio/practical labs, structured theory sessions and an industry practicum—culminating in assessment, certification and pathways to internships or micro-business support.

Practitioner-led workshops and project supervision
Work placement, micro-internship and local enterprise links
Portfolio development and end-of-course certification
